Running the innovation jury

How to build your panels, schedule the sessions and collect the scores — and what every button on the screen actually does.

Your console: smartmarinaconnect.com/sm26/yv  ·  Yacht Club de Monaco, 20–21 September 2026

How it works

You don't assign one juror to one startup. You work in groups, and the platform does the bookkeeping underneath.

Jury panelA group of jurors, mixed by type. JG1, JG2, …
×
Startup batchThe startups heard together. SG1, SG2, …
→
SlotOne panel meets one batch, at one time.
What happens automaticallyThe moment a panel is paired with a batch, every juror on that panel gets a scorecard for every startup in that batch. You never create scorecards by hand.

Sizes are entirely yours. A panel can hold three jurors or seven; a batch can hold four startups or eight. Nothing forces a particular number.

The one thing to remember

Nothing is ever emailed until you click a button that says so. Creating panels, batches and slots is completely silent — you can build the whole timetable, look at it, change your mind and rebuild it, without a single message leaving.

When you are ready, three separate clicks send three emails, in this order:

  1. Send availability request — asks the panel whether the slot works for them.
  2. Send Zoom invite — creates the meeting and sends the calendar invitation.
  3. Email jurors to score — after the session, asks them for their feedback forms.

Step by step

1

Tag your jurors  — Jurors tab

For each juror, set their Type (Angel, VC or Corporate) and their Owner (YV or M3). Type is what lets the console warn you when a panel isn't properly mixed. Owner is simply a reminder of who holds the relationship.

A juror only appears in the panel picker once they have a platform account and M3 has confirmed their jury role. If someone you expect is missing, that is almost always why — ask M3 to confirm them.

2

Build your panels  — Jury panels tab

New panel creates JG1, then JG2, and so on. Add jurors from the dropdown, which shows each person's type and how many panels they already sit on. You can rename a panel to anything you find clearer.

A panel is flagged amber in only three cases: it has no jurors yet, one of its members has no Type set, or everyone on it is the same type. Its size is never questioned.

3

Build your batches  — Startup batches tab

New batch creates SG1, SG2, and so on. The picker shows each startup's stage and tells you if it already sits in another batch. The heading keeps a running count of how many startups aren't in a batch yet.

4

Put them on the timetable  — Timetable tab

Two ways to do this:

Add a slot — choose a panel, a batch, a date and time, and a length. One slot at a time.

Auto-build — give it a first slot, a length and a number of rounds, and it proposes a whole rotation in which each panel meets a different batch each round. Review the proposal, untick anything you don't want, then create.

Either way you get Draft slots. No Zoom, no email. The scorecards behind them already exist.

5

Ask the panel if the slot works

Send availability request emails each juror on that panel, with two buttons: Confirm availability and Not available. They don't need to log in — one click is enough.

As answers arrive, the slot shows 2/3 available and each juror's pill turns green or red. Nobody is chased automatically; you can re-send whenever you like.

6

Send the Zoom invite

When you're happy with the answers, Send Zoom invite. This is the moment the meeting is actually created. The calendar invitation goes to the jurors who confirmed, to the batch's startups, and to you and Victor.

Each person's invitation lists only themselves, so the startups in a batch never see one another's email addresses.

If nobody has answered yet, the button says so and asks you to confirm before it sends anyway.

7

After the session, collect the scores

Email jurors to score sends every juror on the panel a link to their scorecards, listing the startups they just heard. They score at their own pace, and the slot shows the progress — 4/18 scored — as it comes in.

The state of a slot

Draft — nothing sentCreated, and the scorecards behind it exist, but no email has left and there is no meeting.
Availability requestedThe panel has been asked. Answers appear as they come in.
Zoom invite sentThe meeting exists and the calendar invitation is out.
All scores inEvery juror has submitted a scorecard for every startup in the batch.

The small buttons on a slot

Clock icon — rescheduleOnly while a slot is still a draft. Once the Zoom invitation is out, cancel the session instead and create a new one.
Bin icon — delete draftRemoves a slot that never went anywhere. Nothing was sent, so nobody is notified.
✕ — cancel sessionFor a live session: deletes the Zoom meeting and emails everyone a cancellation, which removes it from their calendar.

The Innovations tab

One row per startup. Click it and a panel slides in with everything about that entry: which batch it's in, which panels review it and when, each assigned juror's answer and whether they've scored, plus its registration and payment status. You can add a single extra juror here for a one-off — but normal assignment should happen through panels and batches.

Trying it out without emailing anyone

Two safety nets, and you can use either as much as you like.

“preview to me” links At the bottom right of any slot. They send that exact email — availability, Zoom invite or scoring request — to you and nobody else, so you can see what a juror will receive.
TEST slots Tick Test slot when adding a slot. It is marked [TEST] and physically refuses to email anyone except you, whatever you click. Its send buttons are hidden; use the preview links.

Worth knowing

TimesEverything shown on screen and written in the emails is Monaco time (CET/CEST). You don't need to convert anything.
LanguageThe jury scoring screens are in English.
What jurors seeAt smartmarinaconnect.com/sm26/jury: their own sessions, who else is on their panel, the startups they'll hear, their Zoom link once it's sent, and their scorecards.
Changing an answerA juror can change their availability at any time, either from the email link or from that page.
The official rankingLives with M3 in the admin console. It's built from the submitted scorecards — the same ones your panels fill in.
You're not alone in hereM3 can see and edit the same panels, batches and timetable. If a juror is already assigned to an entry, that's usually why.
