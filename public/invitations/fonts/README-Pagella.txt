Pagella-Regular.ttf and Pagella-Italic.ttf
==========================================

These two files are TeX Gyre Pagella (font family "TeX Gyre Pagella", PostScript names
TeXGyrePagella-Regular and TeXGyrePagella-Italic, version 2.501), copyright 2006-2018 for
TeX Gyre extensions by B. Jackowski, J.M. Nowacki et al. (on behalf of TeX USERS GROUPS).

They are distributed under the GUST Font License (GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt in this folder,
which refers to the LaTeX Project Public License 1.3c or later).

Conversion note: the files were converted from the original OpenType (CFF) release to
TrueType outlines and renamed Pagella-*.ttf, so that the invitation PDFs can embed them with
pdf-lib and fontkit (subset: false). The conversion was meant to change the outline format
only; the internal font names above were kept. They are used only to draw the invitation
tickets and letters of the Smart Marina Connect admin (World Yachting Summit first).
